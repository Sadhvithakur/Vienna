## 🧪 Quick Test Checklist

### On Desktop (Zoom to 100%)
- [ ] Hamburger is **not visible**
- [ ] Menu shows **horizontally**
- [ ] Resize down to 768px → hamburger appears

### On Mobile (or zoom to <1024px)
- [ ] Click hamburger → slides down smoothly
- [ ] Hamburger animates to **X** shape
- [ ] Click outside menu → closes
- [ ] Press **ESC** key → closes
- [ ] Click any menu item → closes + scrolls
- [ ] Cannot scroll page while menu is open
